Biography Data

Name: Enya
Age: N/A
Race: Andro-Eidos (Prototype)

Data:
Contrary to what many have come to believe, while Liandri Corporation may have recovered the blueprints from New Solos, much was still left to the unknown. This meant that the great minds at Liandri still had to piece together the more technical parts of the androids, resulting in multiple internal versions being built.

One aspect that was set from the start however, was to keep their appearance as much as possible, resembling the humans their dark past has always intended them to replace. Several years of Research and Development, Liandri built a multitude of 1st to 5th generation Andro-Eidos Androids, many of whom were merely stepping stones for what would later be introduced to the Tournament.

This specific model was one of the first combat "Protype" androids Liandri focused on, built specifically for the League, codenamed "Enya". It was built on the previous generations' framework, but with a lot more of Liandri's proprietary hardware. Despite the intent, due to under performing in initial tests, the prototype was delayed for public release to the League, and focus was shifted towards building the 6th and 7th generation Andro-Eidos that would be inducted into the League due in part to the valuable data Liandri received from the prototype's controlled field tests.

Now, years after Raven and Tachy models were unveiled, and receiving high demand for more from millions of Tournament Spectators, Liandri has dedicated resources towards fully completing this prototype Andro.
